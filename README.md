# The Word You'd Delete From the Dictionary

One word. That's the whole entry: **no**.

Erase it and nobody on Earth can refuse anything ever again. Toddlers can't
say it to broccoli. Parents can't say it to "can I have a dog?". Bouncers
can't say it at the door, bosses can't say it to a raise, and every
telemarketer on the planet suddenly becomes unstoppable — because the word
that stops things is gone.

It's the smallest word with the biggest hole left behind. Two letters, and
the entire planet loses its brakes. Humanity had exactly one emergency stop
button, and this is it.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib-only, deterministic) writes the single word to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. No network, no secrets, no input files.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt  # no
```
